# A Simple Weekly Planning System for Busy People

When your week is full, planning can feel like another task on an already long list. A lightweight weekly system can make everything clearer without adding complexity.

## Start with One Weekly Reset

Pick one consistent time each week, such as Sunday evening or Monday morning. During this reset, review what is coming up and decide what matters most.

Focus on three things:

- Deadlines and appointments
- Tasks that move important projects forward
- Personal and household responsibilities

## Use Three Priority Buckets

Instead of one long task list, sort your week into three buckets:

- Must do
- Should do
- Nice to do

This makes it easier to protect your time when the week gets busy.

## Plan by Theme, Not by Hour

Assign simple themes to days when possible. For example:

- Monday: admin and communication
- Tuesday: project work
- Wednesday: home and errands

Themes reduce decision fatigue and make your schedule easier to follow.

## Keep a Daily Top Three

At the start of each day, select only three high-value tasks. Complete these first before adding extra work.

A daily top-three list helps you finish meaningful work even on unpredictable days.

## Review and Improve Weekly

At the end of the week, spend five minutes reviewing what worked and what did not. Adjust your approach for next week.

Small weekly improvements make your planning system stronger over time.

---

**Key Takeaway:** A simple weekly planning routine gives you structure, reduces overwhelm, and helps you stay consistent across work and everyday life.
