Inventory is one of the quiet places where restaurant money disappears. A few inaccurate counts, rushed orders, and forgotten products can gradually create overstock, spoilage, emergency purchases, or stockouts during service.

Good inventory management creates a repeatable view of what is available, what is running low, and what must be reordered. That visibility helps managers buy what the business needs instead of ordering extra stock just to be safe.

## Where consistent counts save money

- Reduce over-ordering and tied-up cash
- Find slow-moving stock before it expires
- Prevent service disruptions caused by stockouts
- Improve par levels and purchasing discipline
- Make inventory tasks easier to delegate and review

The best starting system is usually simple: count on a fixed schedule, use the same categories every time, flag reorder items, and keep past records available for comparison. Consistency turns inventory from a recurring surprise into a manageable routine.
