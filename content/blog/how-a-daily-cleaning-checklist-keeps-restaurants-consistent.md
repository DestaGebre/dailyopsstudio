Cleaning tasks are often familiar, but familiarity does not guarantee consistency. When responsibilities are informal, the same areas can be missed repeatedly and supervisors have little evidence that the work was completed.

A daily cleaning checklist turns expectations into an observable routine. It can group tasks by dining area, kitchen, equipment, restrooms, and waste disposal while giving staff and supervisors a place to confirm completion.

## What the record improves

- Clear ownership of daily tasks
- More consistent standards between shifts
- Faster supervisor verification
- Easier onboarding for new staff
- A written history of completed work

Keep the form simple enough to use during a real shift. Define the task, frequency, responsible person, and verification step. If a problem needs more than routine cleaning, record it for maintenance or manager follow-up.
