# A Weekly Operations Review for Small Businesses

Most business problems are easier to fix when you spot them early. A weekly operations review helps you do that in under an hour.

## What to Review Each Week

Use one checklist and repeat it every week:

- Sales, leads, and conversion trends
- Delivery timelines and open customer issues
- Team workload and upcoming deadlines
- Cash flow, invoices, and outstanding payments
- Process bottlenecks that slowed work this week

## Turn Notes into Next Actions

End each section with one clear action owner and deadline. Reviews only work when decisions become visible tasks.

## Track Improvements Over Time

Keep each weekly review in a shared log. After a few weeks, patterns become obvious and improvements are easier to prioritize.

---

**Key Takeaway:** A weekly operations review turns scattered updates into a reliable process that improves decision-making and execution.
