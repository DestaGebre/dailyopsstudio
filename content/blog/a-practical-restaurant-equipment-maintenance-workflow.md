Equipment problems become expensive when they are reported informally. Repeated faults, delayed repairs, and unclear ownership can interrupt service and make it difficult to understand what has already been tried.

A maintenance log should connect the first report to final sign-off. Record the equipment, fault, urgency, operational impact, immediate action, service call, responsible person, and follow-up date.

## A simple issue lifecycle

1. Identify the equipment and describe the fault clearly.
2. Assess urgency, safety risk, and impact on service.
3. Record any immediate action or temporary workaround.
4. Assign the service call or internal follow-up.
5. Verify the result and close the issue with a date and name.

One consistent record makes unresolved problems visible and helps managers distinguish isolated faults from recurring maintenance patterns.
