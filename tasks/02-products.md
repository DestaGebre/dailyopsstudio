# Task 02 — Product Content System

## Objective

Create a Markdown-driven product catalogue.

## Work

1. Define the Nuxt Content product collection.
2. Create a reusable product Markdown schema.
3. Add ProductCard.vue.
4. Add ProductGrid.vue.
5. Build the shop page.
6. Build dynamic product detail pages.
7. Add category filtering.
8. Add external Etsy purchase buttons.
9. Add empty and error states.

## Initial categories

- Business Operations
- Home Organization
- Personal Planning
- Vehicle Management
- Events and Checklists

## Acceptance criteria

- Products are loaded from Markdown.
- Product pages are generated dynamically.
- Etsy links open safely.
- The design works on mobile.
- Typecheck and build pass.
